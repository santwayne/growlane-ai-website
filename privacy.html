Privacy Policy

How Growlane AI collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us through our forms — such as your name, email address, phone number, business name and message — and basic usage data (pages visited, device and browser type) to improve the website.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and demo requests
  • To provide and improve our services
  • To send updates or newsletters you have subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To keep our website secure

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with trusted service providers who help us run our business (for example hosting or email providers), and only as needed to provide our services or when required by law.

4. Cookies

We may use essential cookies and analytics to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data and keep it only as long as necessary for the purposes above.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
